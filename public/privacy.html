Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This notice explains how Alkarim Packages handles information when you visit this website or contact us about packaging. It applies to this website; third-party services have their own privacy notices.

Who is responsible

Alkarim Packages is the business responsible for the enquiries it receives through the contact details on this website. Contact us at alkarimpackages@gmail.com, by telephone on +92 300 2659004, or at 45/1, 2, Sector 12 D Ind, Karachi, 75400, Pakistan.

Information we receive

If you email or call us, we receive the contact information and project details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number, box dimensions, quantities and correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information or payment card details. This website has no account registration, checkout, file upload or message-submission form.

Our hosting infrastructure may process your IP address, browser information, requested address, request time and technical errors to deliver and secure the website. The hosting provider must be identified before this website is launched.

How and why we use information

We use enquiry information to respond, discuss requirements, prepare quotations and take steps towards a contract at your request. We use relevant order records to perform a contract and meet legal obligations. Where applicable data protection law requires a lawful basis, technical security and essential business administration rely on our legitimate interests in operating a safe, reliable service, subject to your rights. Optional analytics relies on consent. We do not use this website to build advertising profiles or make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Cookies and browser storage

The website stores your cookie choice in local storage under alkarim-consent-v1, with a choice, version and timestamp, for up to 180 days. This is necessary to remember your preference. If storage is blocked, the choice applies only to the current page. You can change your choice using Cookie settings in the footer. Essential website access does not depend on accepting analytics.

Optional analytics is not enabled on this website. No analytics or advertising tags load. If analytics is introduced, this notice will identify the provider, purpose and retention before collection starts.

The 3D studio generates images locally in your browser. Using Capture view or Save composition does not upload the image to us. Enabling the studio does not consent to analytics.

Sharing and external services

We share information only as needed with service providers that operate hosting, business email and communications, professional advisers, and authorities where legally required. We do not sell personal information. Email links open your chosen email application, telephone links open your calling application, and Get directions opens Google Maps. These services may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

International processing

Our business is in Pakistan. Email, hosting and any enabled analytics providers may process information in other countries. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, those safeguards must be put in place, such as recognised adequacy decisions or appropriate contractual protections. Contact us to ask about the arrangements relevant to your information.

How long information is kept

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and manage the business relationship. Relevant contracts, invoices and correspondence may be retained for applicable tax, accounting, legal or dispute-resolution requirements. We assess the purpose, sensitivity and applicable obligations when deciding retention, and delete or anonymise information that is no longer needed. The cookie-choice retention period is stated above. Hosting log retention must be confirmed before public launch.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to certain processing. You may withdraw analytics consent at any time through Cookie settings without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Rejecting analytics stops future optional collection; deletion of previously collected records may require a separate request.

Send privacy requests to alkarimpackages@gmail.com. We may need proportionate information to verify your identity. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You may also complain to the relevant data protection authority; if EU or UK data protection law applies to you, this includes your local supervisory authority.

Security and children

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the information involved. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. This website is intended for business enquiries and is not directed at children. If a child has provided information, contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our services or practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes to optional data collection will require updated information and, where appropriate, a new consent choice.
